How to Hire AEC Professionals Faster: A Guide for Employers

By Castallio One Team • Published 30 Sept 2026 •Updated 10 Oct 2026 • 1 min read

To hire AEC professionals quickly, you need to be specific about what you want and make it easy for candidates to respond. Specialised roles take longer to fill when the job post is vague.

Write a precise job post

Name the software, discipline, project type and experience range. Compare these two titles: Engineer needed, and Revit MEP Coordinator with 3 to 5 years on commercial projects. The second attracts the right people and filters out the rest.

Use walk-in drives for volume

Walk-in drives let you meet many qualified candidates in a single day. Share the venue, time and documents required clearly, so candidates arrive prepared.

Match on skills, not keywords

Look for verified software and technical skills on each profile. Skill-based matching surfaces candidates you might miss when searching by job title alone.

Keep communication in one place

  • Message shortlisted candidates directly.
  • Schedule interviews with clear time slots.
  • Track every application status in one dashboard.

Final thoughts

Faster hiring comes from clarity. Write precise posts, respond quickly and keep the process simple for candidates.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a good job post for an AEC role?

List the exact software, discipline, project type and experience range so matching works on specifics.

What is a walk-in drive?

A walk-in drive is a hiring event where qualified candidates meet your team in person on a set day.
