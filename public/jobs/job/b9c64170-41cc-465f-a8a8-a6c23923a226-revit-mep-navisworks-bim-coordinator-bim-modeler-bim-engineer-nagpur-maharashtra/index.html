Job description

BIM ENGINEER

*Company:* Prism Infratech Pvt. Ltd.

*Position:* BIM Engineer

*Experience:* 2–3 Years

*Number of Openings:* 4

*Location:* Nagpur, Maharashtra

*Joining:* Immediate Joiners Preferred

About Prism Infratech

Prism Infratech is a BIM consultancy company based in India, delivering engineering and BIM solutions for projects across India and international markets.

Job Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain accurate 3D BIM models using Autodesk Revit.

- Prepare detailed 2D drawings, sections, elevations and documentation.

- Coordinate with architectural, structural and MEP teams.

- Review models and identify design clashes and inconsistencies.

- Follow project standards, BIM guidelines and client requirements.

- Support project delivery within defined timelines.

- Coordinate with senior engineers and project managers.

Required Skills

- Good working knowledge of Autodesk Revit.

- Knowledge of AutoCAD and Navisworks.

- Understanding of BIM workflows and modelling standards.

- Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings.

- Good coordination and communication skills.

- Strong attention to detail and problem-solving abilities.

Eligibility Criteria

- Diploma / Bachelor's Degree in Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering or Architecture.

- 2–3 years of relevant BIM experience.

- Candidates who can join immediately will be preferred.
