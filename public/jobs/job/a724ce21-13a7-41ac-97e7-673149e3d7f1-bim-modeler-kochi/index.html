Job Summary

We are seeking a skilled Architectural BIM Modeler to develop, coordinate, and maintain architectural BIM models throughout project lifecycles. The ideal candidate should possess strong expertise in Revit-based modeling, architectural detailing, rendering, and shop drawing production while ensuring compliance with BIM standards and project requirements.

Key Responsibilities

• Develop and maintain Architectural BIM models from LOD 400 to LOD 500.

• Create detailed architectural shop drawings including plans, sections, elevations, schedules, and detailing sheets.

• Produce high-quality renderings and visualizations for client presentations and design reviews.

• Participate in multidisciplinary BIM coordination meetings.

• Perform clash detection and resolve coordination issues with Structural and MEP teams.

• Ensure model quality and compliance with BIM Execution Plans (BEP).

• Update models based on design revisions and site feedback.

• Support QA/QC processes and model audits.

• Maintain BIM documentation and project standards.

Required Skills

• Strong proficiency in Revit Architecture.

• Experience with Navisworks Manage.

• Knowledge of Enscape, Lumion, Twinmotion, or similar rendering software.

• Understanding of architectural materials, finishes, and construction detailing.

• Familiarity with BIM workflows and LOD 350–500 requirements.

• Ability to generate detailed shop drawings and construction documentation.

Qualification

• Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Architecture or related field.
