Job description

**Job Summary**

Kiranotech Engineering Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is looking for an experienced and detail-oriented Revit RCC Detailer with 5–10 years of experience in Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) detailing and BIM modelling. The candidate should have strong expertise in developing detailed structural reinforcement models, reinforcement drawings and construction documentation using Autodesk Revit.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Develop detailed RCC structural BIM models using Autodesk Revit.

- Prepare reinforcement detailing drawings for beams, columns, slabs, foundations, shear walls and other RCC elements.

- Prepare reinforcement layouts, sections, elevations and Bar Bending Schedules (BBS).

- Interpret structural design drawings and reinforcement specifications.

- Ensure accurate reinforcement modelling, bar placement, spacing, cover and lap lengths.

- Prepare detailed shop drawings and construction drawings.

- Coordinate with structural engineers, BIM coordinators and other discipline teams.

- Identify and resolve reinforcement clashes and detailing discrepancies.

- Maintain project-specific BIM standards and detailing requirements.
