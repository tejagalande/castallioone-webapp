Revit Modeler (Architectural / Structural)

Quick answer: Revit Modeler (Architectural / Structural) at Castallio Enterprise Partner in Amritsar, Maharashtra. Posted 2026-11-26; apply by 2026-12-26.

Castallio Enterprise Partner · Amritsar, Maharashtra · Posted 2026-11-26

Job description

Kiranotech Engineering Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is looking for skilled and detail-oriented Revit Modelers specializing in Architectural and Structural modelling. The ideal candidate should have at least 2 years of hands-on experience in Autodesk Revit and should be capable of developing accurate BIM models and construction drawings.

Responsibilities:

Develop and maintain Architectural and Structural BIM models using Autodesk Revit.

Prepare detailed 2D drawings, plans, sections, elevations and construction documentation.

Coordinate Architectural and Structural models.

Understand and interpret architectural and structural drawings.

Maintain modelling accuracy and follow project BIM standards.

Coordinate with BIM Coordinators and engineering teams.

Identify and resolve modelling discrepancies.

Requirements:

Minimum 2 years of hands-on experience in Autodesk Revit.

Practical experience in Architectural and Structural BIM Modelling.

Good understanding of Architectural and Structural drawings.

Ability to develop 3D BIM models, plans, sections, elevations and detailed drawings.

Knowledge of Revit Families, Levels, Grids and View Templates.

Understanding of BIM standards and modelling practices.

Working knowledge of AutoCAD.

Ability to coordinate Architectural and Structural models and resolve modelling discrepancies.

Diploma or Degree in Civil Engineering, Architecture or a relevant discipline.
