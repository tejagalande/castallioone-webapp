Job description

We are looking for a enthusiastic and detail-oriented BIM Engineer with 0-1 years of hands-on experience to join our technical team in Pune. In this role, you will be responsible for creating, managing, and coordinating multidisciplinary 3D BIM models (Architectural, Structural) throughout various project stages.

You will collaborate closely with Architects, Structural Engineers, , and Project Managers to ensure design accuracy, conduct clash detection, and produce construction-ready documentation in compliance with ISO 19650 standards and project BIM Execution Plans (BEP).

Responsibilities:

3D BIM Modeling: Create and update accurate Architectural, Structural, or MEP models in Autodesk Revit as per project guidelines.

Clash Resolution: Run clash detection tests in Navisworks, identify design conflicts, and coordinate with teams to resolve them.

Drawing Extraction: Generate accurate 2D CAD drawings, floor plans, sections, and material schedules directly from BIM models.

Model Quality Checks: Perform quality control on models to ensure correct naming conventions, worksets, and modeling standards.

Team Coordination: Upload and manage project files on Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC/BIM 360) and support Senior BIM Coordinators.

Requirements:

Autodesk Revit (Architecture / Structure /):

Strong proficiency in 3D modeling, central file worksharing, worksets, and basic parametric family creation.

Autodesk Navisworks Manage

Hands-on experience with clash detection, clash matrix setup, and report generation.

Proficient in 2D drafting, CAD cleanup, and CAD-to-BIM conversion.
