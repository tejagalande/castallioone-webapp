# Electrical BIM Modeler / BIM Coordinator

**Company:** Tejsheel Private Limited (Tejsheel BIM Studio)

**Job Location:** Pune, Maharashtra

**Employment Type:** Full-Time

**Experience:** 2–5 Years (Preferred)

**Project Type:** US Electrical BIM – LOD 400

**Department:** BIM – Electrical

**Work Mode:** As per company requirements

## About Tejsheel BIM Studio

Tejsheel BIM Studio is a Pune-based BIM consultancy providing Building Information Modelling services across Architectural, Structural, and MEPF disciplines. We deliver coordinated, construction-ready BIM models and documentation for projects in India and international markets.

We are looking for a skilled and detail-oriented **Electrical BIM Modeler / BIM Coordinator** to join our team and work on US-based electrical projects. The candidate will be responsible for developing LOD 400 electrical BIM models, coordinating electrical systems with other disciplines, and preparing accurate, installation-ready shop drawings.

## Key Responsibilities

* Develop and maintain electrical BIM models in Autodesk Revit based on contract drawings, engineering drawings, redlines, markups, feeder matrices, and project specifications.

* Model electrical conduits, conduit racks, cable trays, electrical equipment, hangers, supports, and associated electrical systems.

* Develop coordinated conduit routing and consolidate conduit racks to optimize installation and minimize clashes.

* Perform model coordination and clash detection using Autodesk Navisworks.

* Coordinate electrical systems with Architectural, Structural, Mechanical, Plumbing, and other relevant disciplines.

* Prepare coordinated electrical shop drawings, including:

* Conduit layout drawings.

* Conduit rack and hanger layout drawings.

* Electrical equipment layout drawings.

* Lighting layout drawings.

* Penetration drawings.

* Other electrical installation and coordination drawings.

* Interpret engineering drawings, redlines, markups, and technical specifications independently.

* Identify discrepancies, modelling constraints, and coordination issues and seek clarification when required.

* Maintain model accuracy, drawing quality, and compliance with project requirements.

* Provide daily progress updates and communicate proactively with project stakeholders.

* Respond promptly to project communications through email and Microsoft Teams.

* Meet agreed production schedules, submission deadlines, and quality standards.

* Maintain strict confidentiality and comply with project-specific information security requirements.
