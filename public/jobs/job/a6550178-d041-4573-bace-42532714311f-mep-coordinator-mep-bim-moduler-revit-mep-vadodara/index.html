Job description

JOB DESCRIPTION – BIM MEP COORDINATOR

Location: Vadodara , Gujrat

Department: BIM – MEP

Experience: 2–5 Years

Employment Type: Full-Time

Number of Openings: 20

Job Summary

Prism Infratech is looking for skilled and experienced BIM MEP Coordinators to join our growing engineering team. The candidate will be responsible for managing MEP BIM coordination, developing and reviewing BIM models, identifying clashes, and ensuring accurate project deliverables.

Key Responsibilities

Develop and coordinate MEP BIM models using Autodesk Revit.

Perform clash detection and clash resolution using Navisworks.

Coordinate HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing and Firefighting systems.

Review BIM models and ensure compliance with project requirements.

Prepare and review shop drawings and construction drawings.

Coordinate with architectural, structural and MEP teams.

Conduct BIM coordination meetings and resolve technical issues.

Perform model quality checks (QA/QC).

Maintain BIM standards, LOD requirements and project documentation.

Ensure timely delivery of BIM models and project deliverables.

Required Skills & Qualifications

Diploma or Bachelor's degree in Mechanical, Electrical or Civil Engineering.

2–5 years of experience in BIM MEP coordination.

Strong knowledge of Autodesk Revit MEP.

Hands-on experience with Navisworks Manage.

Knowledge of HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing and Firefighting systems.

Understanding of LOD 300, 350 and 400.

Knowledge of clash detection, coordination and BIM workflows.

Good communication, teamwork and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Skills

Experience working on international BIM projects.

Knowledge of BIM standards and documentation.

Experience in multidisciplinary coordination.
