Job description

Job Overview:

UBP.ONE is looking for a SketchUp Modeler with around 1- 2 years of experience to join our growing team. The ideal candidate should have a basic understanding of 3D modeling, architectural drawings, and visualization using SketchUp. This role involves creating accurate 3D models based on design concepts, CAD drawings, and project requirements.

Key Responsibilities:

Create and modify 3D models using SketchUp based on architectural or design drawings.

Convert 2D drawings into detailed 3D models.

Apply basic materials, textures, and components to models.

Collaborate with designers, architects, and project teams to understand modeling requirements.

Maintain organized model files and project documentation.

Make revisions to models based on feedback from the team.

Ensure models are accurate and aligned with project specifications.

Required Skills:

Basic to intermediate knowledge of SketchUp.

Understanding of architectural drawings and 3D visualization.

Knowledge of AutoCAD (basic) is an added advantage.

Good attention to detail and accuracy.

Ability to work in a team environment and meet deadlines.

Qualifications:

Diploma / Bachelor’s Degree in Architecture, Interior Design, Civil Engineering, or related field.

1-2 years of experience in SketchUp modeling or similar 3D software.

Preferred Skills (Optional):

Knowledge of V-Ray, Lumion, or Enscape for rendering.

Basic understanding of interior or architectural design concepts.

Responsibilities:

Your Role:

Create high-quality 3D models using SketchUp for interior spaces, furniture, fixtures, and products.

Develop accurate 3D models based on architectural drawings, design concepts, and project requirements.

Understand interior layouts, materials, dimensions, textures, and design details to ensure modeling accuracy.

Collaborate with architects, interior designers, and BIM teams to deliver design-focused 3D models.

Prepare detailed 3D models for design development, visualization, and client presentations.

Work with supporting software such as AutoCAD, 3ds Max, and Blender.

Use rendering and visualization software such as V-Ray, Corona Renderer, D5 Render, Enscape, Lumion, or other relevant rendering tools.

Create realistic interior renders with proper lighting, materials, textures, and detailing.

Develop 3D walkthrough animations and camera sequences for interior spaces and architectural projects.

Requirements:

Hands-on experience with rendering tools such as V-Ray, Corona Renderer, D5 Render, Enscape, or Lumion.
