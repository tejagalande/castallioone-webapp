Job description

About Us:

At UBP we are a leading BIM service provider working on diverse projects across the Sweden, UK, UAE, France, Australia, Netherlands and Canada. We specialize in delivering high-quality architectural modelling, restoration, facility management, and Scan-to-BIM services. Join our growing team of passionate professionals dedicated to shaping the future of digital construction.

Job Description:

We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Junior BIM Architect with a minimum of 1 year of hands-on experience in Autodesk Revit.

The ideal candidate should have an academic background in B.Arch., Diploma in Architecture, or a related field in the AEC industry.

Required Skills & Qualifications:

 Educational Qualification: B.Arch. / Diploma in Architecture / related field in the AEC industry.

 Minimum 1 year of working experience in Autodesk Revit.

 Sound understanding of architectural modelling standards and BIM workflows.

 Proficient in topography modelling and working with site-related elements.

 Experience in working with central files and BIM 360 for team collaboration.

 Knowledge of sheet setup, detailing, and annotation standards.

 Familiarity with phases and design options in Revit.

 Strong attention to detail and a commitment to high-quality deliverables.

 Preferred Qualifications (Good to Have)

 Understanding of AutoCAD Software.

 Experience in international projects (US/UK/UAE/Australia/Canada) is advantage.

Responsibilities:

Key Responsibilities:

 Develop accurate and detailed architectural BIM models using Autodesk Revit.

 Model topography and architectural elements based on provided data and project requirements.

 Understand and follow architecture discipline standards and coordination workflows.

 Work efficiently in a central model environment and collaborate through BIM 360.

 Prepare sheet layouts, annotations, and presentation drawings according to project standards.

 Handle phasing work for renovation/restoration and new construction projects.

 Coordinate with project leads and follow defined BIM execution plans.

Requirements:

Technical & Modeling Skills

• Architectural Modeling: Building intelligent 3D components—including walls, curtain walls, floors, roofs, ceilings, and stairs—with correct parametric constraints.

• Family & Object Creation: Developing custom parametric 2D/3D BIM families and components.

• Documentation Production: Extracting accurate plans, sections, elevations, schedules, and detailed construction sheets straight from the model.

• Data Management: Embedding non-graphic metadata (such as material specs, fire ratings, and manufacturer data) and exporting schedules to Excel.

• Quality Control (QC): Performing model audits, checking worksets, managing file naming conventions, and cleaning up views
