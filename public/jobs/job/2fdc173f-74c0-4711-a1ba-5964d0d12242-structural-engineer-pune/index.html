Structural Engineer

Quick answer: Structural Engineer at Castallio Enterprise Partner in Pune, Maharashtra. Posted 2026-09-28; apply by 2026-10-13.

Castallio Enterprise Partner · Pune, Maharashtra · ₹70,000 - ₹80,000 / month · Posted 2026-09-28

Job description

We are looking for a motivated and detail-oriented **Structural Engineer** with **1–2 years of experience** to join our engineering team. The candidate will be responsible for structural analysis, design, detailing, and coordination of various structural projects while ensuring compliance with applicable codes and standards

Responsibilities:

Perform structural analysis and design for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

* Prepare structural calculations, drawings, and design documentation.

* Develop RCC and steel structural designs as per project requirements.

* Prepare and review structural drawings using AutoCAD and relevant design software.

* Conduct load _calculations and ensure structural stability and safety.

* Coordinate with_ architects, civil engineers, MEP teams, and other project stakeholders.

* Review drawings and identify design or construction-related issues.

* Assist in site inspections and provide technical support during construction.

* Ensure designs comply with applicable codes, standards, and project specifications.

* Maintain accurate engineering documentation and project records.

* Support senior engineers in project planning, design reviews, and technical coordination.

Requirements:

Bachelors degree in **Civil Engineering / Structural Engineering**.

* 1–2 years of relevant experience in structural design and analysis.

* Good knowledge of **RCC and steel structure design**.

* Familiarity with structural analysis and design principles.

* Knowledge of relevant **IS Codes and design standards**.

* Proficiency in **AutoCAD** for structural drawings and detailing.

* Working knowledge of structural design software such as **STAAD.Pro, ETABS, SAFE, or equivalent**.

* Understanding of structural load calculations, load combinations, and design checks.

* Ability to read and interpret architectural and structural drawings.

* Basic knowledge of quantity estimation and preparation of technical documentation.

* Good knowledge of **MS Excel and MS Office**.
