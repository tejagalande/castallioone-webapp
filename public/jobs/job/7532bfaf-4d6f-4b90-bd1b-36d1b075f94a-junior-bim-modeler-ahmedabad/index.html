Junior BIM Modeler

Quick answer: Junior BIM Modeler at Castallio Enterprise Partner in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Posted 2026-11-18; apply by 2026-12-03.

Castallio Enterprise Partner · Ahmedabad, Gujarat · ₹20,000 - ₹20,000 / month · Posted 2026-11-18

Job description

Job Title: Jr. BIM Modeler

Experience: 6 months- 1 years

Education: Diploma or B. E in Civil Engineering

Company: UBP ONE Private Limited (UniquesCadd)

Key Responsibilities:

 Create initial REVIT® Project setup with workflow documents.

 Initiate Model for each project and prepare for production team.

 Provide BIM/REVIT® project kick-off information.

 Work as part of a BIM/CAD support team to achieve consistent results.

 Daily modelling production and do the QC of the productions.

 Provide REVIT® Architecture and BME desktop support.

 Over-the-shoulder training, developing and shadowing to co-team members.

 Content creation: Families such as parametric, formula, nested and schedules.

 Work with architecture disciplines on content received and update them as per the BME’s Standards.

 Support linked views and others in multiple models situation.

 Integration of REVIT® products with other applications (AutoCAD, Takentool, Dynamo).

 Working knowledge of BIM industry workflows, production cycle and priorities.

 Ability to uphold the use of standards and escalate issues in a timely fashion
