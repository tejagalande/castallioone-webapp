Job description

Job Title: BIM Architect

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in Architecture or equivalent qualification.

* Good knowledge of Revit Architecture and BIM workflows.

* Understanding of architectural drawings, construction details, and documentation.

* Ability to develop accurate 3D models and detailed construction drawings.

* Knowledge of BIM standards, families, schedules, and model coordination.

* Good communication, coordination, and problem-solving skills.

* Attention to detail and ability to work within project deadlines.

* Freshers with strong Revit/BIM skills are welcome to apply.

Responsibilities

* Develop and maintain architectural BIM models using Revit.

* Prepare detailed plans, elevations, sections, and construction documentation.

* Create and manage Revit families and project templates where required.

* Coordinate architectural models with structural and MEP disciplines.

* Identify and resolve design and modelling issues.

* Maintain BIM model accuracy, standards, and documentation throughout the project.

* Collaborate with architects, designers, consultants, and project teams.

* Assist in preparing drawings and documentation for construction and client presentations.

What We Offer

* Opportunity to work on diverse architectural and interior design projects.

* Hands-on experience with professional BIM workflows.

* Exposure to real-world design development and construction documentation.

* A creative and collaborative work environment.

* Opportunities to learn, develop, and grow professionally.
