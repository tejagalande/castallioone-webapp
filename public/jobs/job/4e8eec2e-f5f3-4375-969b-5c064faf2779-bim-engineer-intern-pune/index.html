Job description

We are looking for a BIM Intern to support our design and engineering team on live projects. You will work with Revit to develop Architectural and Structural models, create custom families, and help produce coordinated drawings. This is a hands-on role with mentorship and a chance to grow into a full-time position.

Duration: 3–6 months

Responsibilities:

Develop and update Architectural and Structural models in Revit

Create and edit Revit families with proper parameters and types

Assist in preparing sheets, schedules, and views

Run model quality checks and resolve warnings and clashes

Follow BIM standards and naming conventions

Requirements:

Strong working knowledge of Autodesk Revit (Architecture and Structure)

Advanced skills in Family Creation: parametric families, formulas, nested families, and type catalogs
