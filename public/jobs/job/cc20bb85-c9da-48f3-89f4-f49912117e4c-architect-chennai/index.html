Job description

This is a player-coach leadership role. Working directly alongside the Principal Architect, you will own end-to-end design deliverytranslating initial client briefs and concept directions into complete, error-free Good-For-Construction (GFC) drawing packages while mentoring and managing a tight-knit team of two Junior Architects.

Responsibilities:

The role

Work directly with the Principal Architect and own projects from brief to Good-For-Construction (GFC) drawings. You’ll lead two Junior Architects and split your time about 50% drafting and 50% team supervision.

Take projects from concept and 3D massing through design development, material boards and GFC sets.

Assign work, set milestones and review the team’s CAD and SketchUp output.

Coordinate MEP layouts (electrical, HVAC, plumbing, fire), reflected ceiling plans and joinery details.

Prepare client presentations and join design reviews.

Requirements:

Revit & BIM Standards, AutoCAD & Civil 3D , Sketch up , Lumion .
