# Job Description: BIM Engineer

**Company Name:** Prism Infratech Pvt. Ltd.

**Job Title:** BIM Engineer

**Experience Required:** 3–4 Years

**Location:** Nagpur, Maharashtra

**Employment Type:** Full-Time

### Job Summary

Prism Infratech Pvt. Ltd. is looking for a skilled and detail-oriented BIM Engineer with 3–4 years of professional experience in Building Information Modeling (BIM). The candidate will be responsible for developing, managing, and coordinating accurate 3D BIM models, preparing construction documentation, and ensuring effective coordination between different engineering disciplines.

### Key Responsibilities

1. Develop and maintain detailed 3D BIM models for building and infrastructure projects.

2. Create and modify BIM models using Autodesk Revit and other relevant BIM software.

3. Prepare 2D drawings, layouts, sections, elevations, and construction documentation.

4. Coordinate with architectural, structural, and MEP teams.

5. Perform clash detection and resolve model coordination issues.

6. Ensure BIM models comply with project standards and client requirements.

7. Review drawings and models to identify design discrepancies.

8. Collaborate with project managers, engineers, and BIM coordinators.

9. Maintain model quality, accuracy, and project delivery timelines.

### Required Skills & Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Diploma degree in Civil Engineering, Architecture, or a related field.

- 3–4 years of relevant BIM industry experience.

- Strong knowledge of Autodesk Revit.

- Working knowledge of AutoCAD and Navisworks.

- Understanding of BIM workflows, modelling standards, and coordination processes.

- Ability to read and interpret engineering and construction drawings.

- Knowledge of clash detection and BIM coordination.

- Good communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

### Preferred Skills

- Knowledge of Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC).

- Experience working on international BIM projects.

- Knowledge of LOD requirements and BIM execution plans.

- Experience in multidisciplinary BIM coordination.

### What We Offer

- Opportunity to work on international BIM projects.

- Professional learning and career development.

- Collaborative and growth-oriented work environment.

- Opportunity to work with experienced BIM professionals.
