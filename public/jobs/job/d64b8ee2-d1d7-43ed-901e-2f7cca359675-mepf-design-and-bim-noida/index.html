MEPF Design & BIM

Quick answer: MEPF Design & BIM at Castallio Enterprise Partner in Noida, Maharashtra. Posted 2026-10-26; apply by 2026-11-10.

Job description

We are looking for a skilled and detail-oriented MEP Designer to join our engineering team. The candidate will be responsible for preparing MEP layouts, design drawings, BIM models, technical documentation, and coordinated engineering solutions for building and infrastructure projects.

The ideal candidate should have a good understanding of building services, relevant design standards, MEP software, and multidisciplinary coordination workflows.

Responsibilities:

Prepare mechanical, electrical, and plumbing layouts and design drawings as per project requirements.

Develop MEP models and drawings using Revit MEP and AutoCAD.

Work on HVAC, electrical, plumbing, drainage, and fire protection systems, depending on the assigned specialisation.

Prepare coordinated layouts for ducts, cable trays, conduits, pipes, equipment, and other building services.

Develop 3D BIM models and extract 2D drawings, sections, schedules, and documentation.

Coordinate MEP systems with architectural and structural models.

Identify and resolve clashes using Navisworks and other coordination tools.

Review design drawings, specifications, and project requirements.

Support quantity take-offs, equipment schedules, and material calculations where required.

Follow applicable building codes, safety requirements, and project-specific standards.

Coordinate with BIM modellers, design engineers, project coordinators, and other discipline teams.

Maintain model quality, drawing accuracy, and timely project delivery.

Requirements:

Revit MEP and AutoCAD.

Navisworks Manage and Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC).
