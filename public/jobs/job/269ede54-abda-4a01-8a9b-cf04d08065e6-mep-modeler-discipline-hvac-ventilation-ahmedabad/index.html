Job description

Role Summary:

We are seeking a detail-oriented BIM Engineer / BIM Modeler with 1–2 years of experience in HVAC (Ventilation) modeling. The ideal candidate should have a Mechanical Engineering background and hands-on experience in developing accurate BIM models for building services projects. Exposure to international projects will be an added advantage.

Responsibilities:

Key Responsibilities:

• Develop and maintain accurate BIM models for HVAC (Ventilation) systems using Autodesk Revit MEP.

• Prepare coordinated HVAC models, shop drawings, installation drawings, sections, schedules, and other project deliverables.

• Interpret mechanical and construction drawings, specifications, and project documentation to develop accurate BIM models.

• Coordinate HVAC models with other MEP disciplines (Electrical, Plumbing, and Fire Protection) to ensure clash-free designs using Navisworks and BIM coordination workflows.

• Apply knowledge of HVAC systems, including ventilation, ducting, and piping layouts, while modeling and coordinating projects.

• Ensure compliance with BIM standards, project requirements, Level of Development (LOD), and quality control procedures.

• Collaborate with project teams to incorporate design revisions and deliver projects within defined timelines.

Requirements:

An HVAC MEP modeler needs proficiency in 3D BIM and 2D CAD tools—primarily Autodesk Revit MEP, AutoCAD, and Navisworks—alongside practical knowledge of HVAC system components, clash resolution, and shop drawing production
