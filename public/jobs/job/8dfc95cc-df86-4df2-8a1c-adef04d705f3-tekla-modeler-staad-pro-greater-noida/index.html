Job description

We are looking for 5 experienced STAAD Designers to join our dynamic Design & Engineering team. The successful candidates will handle structural design, analysis, and weight estimation of PEB projects using STAAD Pro and related software packages.

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Tekla Modeler to join our team in Greater Noida. The ideal candidate will have 5+ years of hands-on experience in structural steel and PEB modeling, with a proven track record of creating highly accurate 3D models.

Responsibilities:

• Software Expertise: Expert-level, hands-on experience with Tekla Structures is mandatory.

• Domain Knowledge: Deep understanding of structural steel members, PEB modeling, and complex connections.

• Education: Diploma, ITI, B.Tech, or BE in Civil or Mechanical Engineering is preferred.

• Core Competencies: Strong attention to detail, exceptional accuracy, and excellent communication/coordination skills.

• Structural Analysis: Perform comprehensive structural analysis and design of PEB structures utilizing STAAD Pro.

• Calculations & Reporting: Prepare detailed design calculations and comprehensive structural reports adhering to applicable codes.

• Material Optimization: Conduct precise weight estimation and structural optimization of steel structures to ensure cost-effectiveness.

• Component Design: Design primary and secondary steel members, connections, and support systems.

• Verification: Review, verify, and approve structural drawings and fabrication details.

• Team Collaboration: Coordinate seamlessly with detailing, production, and project teams for smooth execution.

• Compliance: Ensure all designs comply with project specifications, safety requirements, and quality standards.

• Technical Support: Resolve design-related issues during the project execution phase and participate in value engineering activities.

Requirements:

STAAD.Pro / SAP2000, Tekla Structures
