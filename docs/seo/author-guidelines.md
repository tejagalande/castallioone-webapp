# Castallio One — AEC Career Editorial & Author Guidelines

**Document:** \`docs/seo/author-guidelines.md\`  
**Target Domain:** \`https://castallioone.com\`  
**Standards:** Google Helpful Content System & Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T)

---

## 1. Editorial Mission & Target Audience

Castallio One publishes technical, objective, and actionable career resources for the Architecture, Engineering & Construction (AEC) industry. Our content serves:
1. **Freshers & Junior Engineers:** Practical roadmaps to transition from academia into real-world civil site, MEP, and BIM modeling roles.
2. **Experienced Professionals:** Senior modeling, clash coordination, project management, and technology adoption advice.
3. **AEC Hiring Managers:** Best practices for writing transparent job requirements and sourcing skilled technical talent.

---

## 2. Core E-E-A-T Editorial Standards

### 2.1 First-Hand Experience & Industry Realism
- Articles must reflect the real day-to-day realities of Indian construction sites, consultancy offices, and BIM studios.
- Avoid abstract or generic advice (e.g. "Work hard and learn software"). Provide concrete specifics: specify tool versions (Revit 2024, Navisworks Manage, Tekla Structures 2023), drawing standards (IS 456, NBC 2016, ISO 19650), and practical deliverables (GFC drawings, BBS, clash reports).

### 2.2 Transparent Authorship & Review Byline
- Every published guide features clear organizational authorship: **Castallio One AEC Career Editorial Team**.
- When an individual industry expert authors or reviews a piece, include their full name, verified credentials (e.g., *B.Tech Civil, Autodesk Certified Professional, 8+ Years BIM Coordination*), and LinkedIn profile link.

### 2.3 Strict Metric & Salary Accuracy
- **NEVER promise or guarantee salaries**: Statements like "BIM Engineers earn ₹15 Lakhs guaranteed" are strictly prohibited.
- Discuss compensation using realistic, transparent ranges segmented by experience (e.g. entry-level 0-2 yrs ₹2.5L - ₹4.5L, mid-level 3-5 yrs ₹5L - ₹8.5L depending on city, software proficiency, and employer size).
- Always note that compensation varies based on project complexity, portfolio evidence, and regional market dynamics.

### 2.4 Neutral, Truthful Zero-Job States
- Content must never invent platform metrics (e.g. "Over 10,000 live jobs on Castallio One").
- When referencing platform opportunities, direct users to dynamic search filters where actual live vacancies can be inspected.

## 3. Article Structure & On-Page Formatting

Each piece must adhere to the standard semantic layout:
1. **H1 Headline:** Clear, user-intent focused, containing the primary keyword naturally.
2. **Introductory Byline & Reading Time:** Includes publication/updated date, estimated read time, and topic category.
3. **Executive Summary / Quick Takeaways:** 3-4 bullet points summarizing the core insights for readers and answer engines.
4. **Logical Section Hierarchy (H2 / H3):** No skipping heading levels. Use descriptive subheadings.
5. **Practical Checklists or Comparison Tables:** Structured tables (e.g., Revit vs Navisworks, Site Engineer vs Planning Engineer responsibilities) that render cleanly on mobile.
6. **Visible FAQ Section (AEO):** Minimum 2 question-and-answer pairs matching user query phrasing.
7. **Official Outbound Resource Citations:** 1-2 links to verified official organizations (buildingSMART, Autodesk, NCS, BIS).
8. **Internal Contextual Job Links:** 1-2 natural links guiding readers to relevant live search categories on Castallio One.

---

## 4. Fact-Checking & 180-Day Review Cycle

- **Pre-Publication Fact Check:** Check all cited software features, standards references, and exam details against current documentation.
- **180-Day Maintenance Schedule:** Every article is cataloged in \`docs/seo/editorial-calendar.csv\` with an explicit refresh due date.
- **Refresh Actions:**
  - Verify that internal job search links still resolve to active categories.
  - Update any obsolete software workflows or discontinued features.
  - Update \`dateModified\` in both page meta and JSON-LD schema ONLY when substantive content revisions are completed.
