## 1. Principles of Natural Link Earning

Search engines reward websites that provide genuine, independent utility. In the AEC job search ecosystem, high-quality links are earned by creating definitive reference tools, worksheets, and career navigation resources that educators, professional societies, and industry leaders want to cite naturally.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Outbound Citations vs. Earned Inbound Backlinks:**
> - When Castallio One links to *buildingSMART*, *Autodesk*, or *NCS India*, that is an **outbound citation**.
> - An **inbound backlink** exists only when an external domain independently places a link pointing to Castallio One.
> - We never mark outreach prospects as "Live Backlinks" until verified via HTTP status checks.

## 2. Core Linkable Assets Suite

We have defined 6 high-utility linkable assets to be published under \`/resources/tools/\` and embedded within our pillar guides:

### Asset 1: The National AEC Roles & Skills Competency Matrix
- **Format:** Interactive table + downloadable high-resolution PDF / spreadsheet.
- **Content:** Breaks down 35+ AEC job roles (BIM Modeler, MEP Coordinator, Site Engineer, Quantity Surveyor, Facade Designer) across experience levels (0-2 yrs, 2-5 yrs, 5+ yrs) detailing expected software proficiencies, site responsibilities, and drawing standards (NBC, IS codes, ISO 19650).
- **Target Audience for Links:** Engineering college placement officers, career counseling blogs, AEC LinkedIn influencers.

### Asset 2: The 15-Point BIM Portfolio Review Checklist
- **Format:** Downloadable PDF + interactive checklist.
- **Content:** Practical guide for freshers and modelers to structure project sheets, LOD representations (LOD 200 vs 350), clash detection reports, and drawing extracts without breaching previous employer NDAs.
- **Target Audience for Links:** Architecture schools, BIM training institutes, design student communities.

### Asset 3: Civil & MEP Site Execution Interview Preparation Pack
- **Format:** Structured web resource with print-friendly layout.
- **Content:** 50 real site scenarios covering concrete slump testing, bar bending schedules (BBS), MEP clash coordination meetings, and contractor billing reconciliations.
- **Target Audience for Links:** Polytechnic and diploma engineering college placement portals, civil engineering student associations.

### Asset 4: Engineering Job Description (JD) Builder for AEC Employers
- **Format:** Interactive template generator.
- **Content:** Helps small-to-midsize architecture and MEP consultancies create clear, compliant, non-discriminatory job posts that specify exact software versions and site requirements.
- **Target Audience for Links:** Regional builder associations (CREDAI local chapters, Builders Association of India - BAI).

### Asset 5: BIM Interoperability & OpenBIM Reference Sheet
- **Format:** Technical explainer + IFC schema reference cheatsheet.
- **Content:** Clear guide on exporting IFC 2x3 and IFC 4 from Revit, Tekla, and ArchiCAD, with common coordinate system alignment workflows.
- **Target Audience for Links:** Academic AEC research pages, openBIM discussion forums.

### Asset 6: Job Scam Awareness & Safe Hiring Guide for Engineering Freshers
- **Format:** Public service announcement resource.
- **Content:** Guide warning candidates against paid interview scams, fake registration fees, and deceptive consultancy contracts, with links to the Ministry of Labour & Employment (NCS India).
- **Target Audience for Links:** College noticeboards, fresher career forums, student advocacy groups.

## 4. Ethical Outreach Execution Guidelines

- **Personalized, Value-First Communication:** Each outreach message highlights how the free resource directly benefits their specific students or readers.
- **No Demands for Reciprocal Links:** The email offers the asset freely for review without requiring a backlink or sponsorship.
- **Data Protection Compliance:** Contact information gathered strictly from official public faculty/career cell directories.
- **Tracking:** Monitored via \`docs/seo/link-outreach-tracker.csv\`.
