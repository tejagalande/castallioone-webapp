Job description

RPJ Design & BIM Solution, Nagpur, is looking for experienced BIM professionals to work on a Data Center Project involving multidisciplinary BIM modeling, coordination, clash detection, and construction documentation.

Responsibilities:

Job Responsibilities – BIM Project Coordination

- Client Coordination: Coordinate with clients to understand project requirements, clarify technical queries, follow up on deliverables, and maintain effective communication.

- Project Reporting: Prepare and share daily, weekly, and monthly project progress reports, including completed work, pending tasks, milestones, and project status.

- Weekly Meetings: Schedule and conduct weekly project coordination meetings with clients and internal teams to discuss progress, challenges, deadlines, and action items.

- Team Management: Manage and coordinate the BIM team, allocate tasks, monitor productivity, and ensure timely project delivery.

- ETM Management: Handle ETM (as per the project's defined ETM process), coordinate with relevant stakeholders, and track updates and pending actions.

- Issue and Risk Management: Identify project issues, coordinate resolutions, maintain issue logs, and follow up on RFIs and technical clarifications.

- Milestone Tracking: Monitor project milestones, submission schedules, client feedback, and approval status.

- Documentation: Maintain meeting minutes (MoM), action trackers, project reports, and other project-related documentation.

- BIM Coordination: Coordinate architectural, structural, and MEPF teams to support model coordination, clash detection, and quality control.

Suggested Job Title: BIM Project Coordinator / BIM Project Engineer

Requirements:

Revit & BIM Standards, Navisworks Clash Detection, ISO 19650 Compliance
